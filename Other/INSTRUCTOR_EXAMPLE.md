## What to learn from it

Look at how the example connects a clearly framed problem to a defined KPI, a benchmark, traceable query results, a chart, a practical recommendation, and stated limitations. Its README also points to the underlying workbook and SQL so the calculations can be followed.

## Keep our project distinct

The instructor example analyzes synthetic Medicare readmissions. Our Team 10 project uses Citi Bike trip data. Use the example as a structural and analytical reference; do not copy its dataset, results, assumptions, or recommendations into the bike-share analysis.
